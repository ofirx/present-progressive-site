Survey of American Literature

American Survey

Explore the voices, ideas, and historical moments that shaped American literature—from early writings and Romanticism to Modernism and contemporary perspectives.

01

Literary Periods

Follow American writing through its major periods and connect each text to its cultural and historical setting.

  • Early American writing
  • Romanticism and Transcendentalism
  • Realism, Modernism, and beyond
02

Central Themes

Compare how different writers respond to recurring questions in American life and identity.

  • Freedom and the American Dream
  • Identity, belonging, and diversity
  • Nature, society, and change
03

Reading Approach

Build a clear interpretation by moving from close reading to historical context and comparison.

  1. Notice key language and imagery.
  2. Connect details to the text’s main idea.
  3. Support your interpretation with evidence.

Discussion question

What does it mean to be American?

As you read, consider how each writer defines—or challenges—American identity.
