The Present Progressive הווה מתמשך (Present Progressive)

Learn how to describe actions happening right now using am / is / are + verb-ing. למדו לתאר פעולות שקורות עכשיו בעזרת am / is / are + verb-ing.

The formula הנוסחה

Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing

I am studying.   She is running.   They are eating.
