Grammar notes כללי דקדוק

Present Progressive — quick guide Formula: Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing Examples: I am studying. She is running. They are eating. Use it for actions happening right now or around now. Negative: am/is/are + not + verb-ing (He is not working.) Questions: Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing? (Are you listening?) Spelling: run → running (double the final consonant)
