Subject–verb agreement

Singular subjects take singular verbs. Plural subjects take plural verbs.

The basic rule

The bird sings.   The birds sing.

With he/she/it, add -s to many present-tense verbs: runs, plays, works.

Tricky phrases between subject and verb

Prepositional phrases do not change the subject's number.

The box of cookies are on the table.

The box of cookies is on the table.

Tip: Cross out the phrase in the middle. Box ... is sounds right.

Compound subjects

Two singular subjects joined by and usually take a plural verb.

Sam and Jordan are ready.

With or / nor, the verb agrees with the subject closest to it.

Neither the coach nor the players were late.
