Apostrophes

Apostrophes show possession or missing letters in contractions. They are small marks with big impact.

Contractions

An apostrophe replaces missing letters.

it's = it is  |  don't = do not  |  we're = we are

Possession

Add 's to most singular nouns. Add ' to plural nouns that already end in s.

the student's notebook (one student)

the students' notebooks (many students)

Its vs. it's

It's always means "it is" or "it has."

Its shows possession and never takes an apostrophe.

The plant lost it's leaves.

The plant lost its leaves.

Quick test: If you can say "it is," use it's. Otherwise use its.
