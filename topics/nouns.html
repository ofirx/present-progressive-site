Nouns & pronouns

Nouns name people, places, things, and ideas. Pronouns stand in for nouns so writing does not sound repetitive.

Common noun types

Common nouns are general: dog, city, idea.

Proper nouns are specific names and are capitalized: Luna, Paris, Monday.

The teacher asked Ms. Rivera a question.

Pronouns replace nouns

Instead of repeating a name, use a pronoun: I, you, he, she, it, we, they.

Maria finished Maria's essay and Maria went home.

Maria finished her essay and she went home.

Make pronouns clear

Readers should know which noun a pronoun refers to.

When Emma met Olivia, she smiled. (Who smiled?)

Emma smiled when she met Olivia.

Tip: Place the pronoun close to the noun it replaces.
