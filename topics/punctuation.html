Punctuation

Punctuation guides readers through your sentences — where to pause, stop, or emphasize.

Periods and question marks

Use a period at the end of a statement. Use a question mark for direct questions.

The meeting starts at nine.

Are you coming to the meeting?

Commas in lists

Separate three or more items with commas.

We packed snacks, water, and maps.

Oxford comma: The comma before "and" can prevent confusion in longer lists.

Commas after introductions

After a short introductory word or phrase, add a comma when it helps clarity.

After dinner we watched a movie.

After dinner, we watched a movie.
