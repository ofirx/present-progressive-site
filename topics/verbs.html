Verbs & tense

Verbs show action or state of being. Tense tells readers when something happens.

Three basic tenses

Past: walked, was, had

Present: walk, is, have

Future: will walk, will be, will have

Yesterday I walked. Today I walk. Tomorrow I will walk.

Keep tense consistent

Switch tenses only when the time really changes.

She opens the door and walked inside.

She opened the door and walked inside.

Helping verbs

Words like is, was, have, and will team up with main verbs.

They have finished the project.

Tip: In questions, helping verbs often move to the front: Have they finished?
